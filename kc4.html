Answer Key — Critical Issues on this Ratecon:
  • Delivery window trap: “Must deliver by Tue 06:00” creates almost no recovery time and opens the door to late penalties.
  • Detention missing: “See Master Agreement” is dangerous. If no Master Agreement exists with clear terms, detention defaults to $0.
  • TONU blank: No cancellation protection. If the load is cancelled, the carrier gets nothing.
  • Late delivery penalty: $250/hour after scheduled time is extreme and was likely never discussed.
  • Lumper language: “Carrier responsible for all lumper fees” shifts cost that should be negotiated or reimbursed.

Correct action: Do not sign. Reply to the broker requesting: (1) realistic delivery window, (2) written detention ($50–$100/hr after 2 free hours), (3) TONU of at least $150–$250, (4) removal or modification of the late penalty, and (5) clarification on lumper reimbursement.
