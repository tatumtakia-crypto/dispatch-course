Module 1: Industry Essentials & Key Terms

Day 1 • Slide 2 of 9

Overview: Master core trucking vocabulary, key industry players, and the strict legal line between dispatching and brokering.

  • Core Vocabulary: FMCSA, DOT Number, MC Number, MOTUS, Load Board, Capacity, Deadhead, Backhaul.
  • Roles & Relationships: Freight Broker vs. Freight Dispatcher vs. Carrier vs. Shipper/Receiver.
  • FMCSA Compliance Rule: Dispatchers represent the carrier under a written agreement and never handle load funds directly. Crossing that line turns you into a broker and requires a $75k surety bond.

Module 2: Business Structure, Tech Stack & Niche

Day 1 • Slide 4 of 9

Overview: Form your LLC, set up essential tech/cloud systems, and choose a profitable equipment niche.

  • Legal & Financial Setup: Form an LLC, obtain a free IRS EIN, open a separate business bank account, and set up merchant processing (Square/Stripe) for weekly invoicing.
  • Cloud Storage & CRM: Structured Google Drive or Dropbox folders per carrier + simple CRM/spreadsheet for leads, MC numbers, and follow-ups.
  • Selecting a Niche: Box Trucks, Dry Vans, Reefers, Flatbeds, specific geographic lanes, or authority age (new vs. seasoned).
  • Brand & Domain: Business name, logo, custom domain + professional email via IONOS (ionos.com).
  • Essential Tech Stack: VoIP phone (OpenPhone / Google Voice), e-signature (DocuSign / PandaDoc), mobile PDF scanner (Adobe Scan).
  • Collateral: One-page service summary, fee schedule (5%–10%), Carrier Packet (Dispatch Agreement + Limited POA).
LLC, tech stack, and niche selection

Module 2: Carrier Onboarding & Risk Verification

Day 2 • Slide 5 of 13

Overview: Collect mandatory documents, verify active MC authority, and avoid cargo-insurance traps.

  • Required Documents: Dispatch Agreement, Limited POA, Active MC/DOT Certificate, Form W-9, COI ($1M Auto / $100k Cargo), Factoring NOA.
  • Authority Status Verification: Always check FMCSA SAFER, directory platforms, and MOTUS on the exact day of booking. Confirm “Active” status.
  • The $100k Cargo Insurance Trap: Compare load value on the Ratecon against the carrier’s maximum cargo coverage before booking high-value freight.

Module 3: Rate Negotiation, Load Boards & Fraud

Day 2 • Slide 8 of 13

Overview: Free/paid load boards, HOS clocks, double-brokering red flags, and negotiating top rates.

  • Free Load Boards: Trucker Path, Freight Finder, Selectus, Trulos, Free Freight Search.
  • Sub-Account Rule: Paid boards (DAT, Truckstop) require an MC/DOT — drivers must add you as an authorized sub-user.
  • HOS & Trip Planning: 11-hour driving limit, 14-hour on-duty window, 34-hour restart, 80,000-lb gross weight.
  • Broker Vetting & Fraud Prevention: Credit scores, $75k surety bond, matching FMCSA email domains.
  • Negotiation: Cost-per-mile floor → lane rate analysis → confident broker pitch.

Module 2: Detention, TONU & Accessorial Routing

Day 3 • Slide 5 of 12

Overview: Enforcing upfront detention and TONU terms and properly logging timestamps.

  • Upfront Ratecon Rule: Detention ($50–$100/hr after 2 free hours) and TONU ($150–$250+) must appear on the initial Ratecon or Master Agreement — otherwise default is $0.
  • Where Details Go: Write exact arrival/departure timestamps on the physical BOL, get receiver signature/stamp, and email the broker immediately on the Ratecon thread.
  • Layovers & Cancellations: Overnight layover fees and TONU claims process.
