Carrier Onboarding Toolkit

Templates + Checklist
Use this toolkit every time you onboard a new carrier. Set up the folder structure, collect every required document, and check off each step. Nothing gets booked until the packet is complete.

Recommended Folder Structure

📁 Carrier Name – MC123456
📁 01 – Contracts
📄 Dispatch Agreement (signed)
📄 Limited Power of Attorney (signed)
📁 02 – Authority & Insurance
📄 MC/DOT Certificate
📄 Certificate of Insurance (COI)
📄 W-9
📁 03 – Factoring
📄 Notice of Assignment (NOA)
📄 Factoring company contact
📁 04 – Ratecons
📁 05 – BOLs & PODs
📁 06 – Invoices (your dispatch invoices)

Documents You Need in the Toolkit

Dispatch Agreement

Services, fee percentage, responsibilities, payment terms, and termination clause.

Required

Limited Power of Attorney

Authorizes you to book loads and sign Ratecons only — no ownership transfer.

Required

COI Requirements Sheet

$1M Auto Liability + minimum $100k Cargo. Always verify limits before high-value freight.
